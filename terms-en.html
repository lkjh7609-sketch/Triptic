Article 1 (Purpose)

These Terms govern the rights, obligations and responsibilities between Triptic (the "Service") and its users in connection with the trip planning, community (destination boards and the free board), companion finder, announcements and user guide features provided by the Service.

Article 2 (Definitions)

  • "Service" means all web/app-based features provided by Triptic, including trip planning, map integration, AI recommendations, sharing, PDF itinerary export, community, companion finder, notifications and announcements.
  • "User" means a member or non-member who accesses and uses the Service under these Terms.
  • "Member" means a person who can continuously use the Service by signing in with Google, Apple or Kakao social login, or by signing up with an email address.
  • "Co-editing member" means a member who signed in through a share link sent by a trip owner (the member who created the trip) and joined that trip. Co-editing members can view and edit the same itinerary as the owner.
  • "Community" means the space where members post titled posts, comments, replies and photos on destination boards and on the free board (which is not tied to any city) and interact with other members. Only members can use it.
  • "Announcements" means posts by the operator about app updates and operating news. They can be read without being a member.
  • "Companion finder" means the feature where a member posts a recruitment post to find travel companions, other members apply, and, once the poster accepts, they are matched as a group. When a match is confirmed, a chat room open only to that group is created, and when they actually meet, they can scan each other's QR codes to confirm they belong to the same group.

Article 7 (Management of Posts)

The rights to and responsibility for itineraries, notes, suggestions, community posts, comments and images, and companion recruitment posts, application messages and chat messages that a user registers on the Service belong to the user who registered that content. In a co-edited trip, what a member enters can be viewed and edited by the trip owner and all members; the owner may remove members, and members may leave at any time. If several members save at the same moment, the content saved first is applied, and the member who saved later is prompted to reload the latest itinerary and enter the changes again. A companion finder application can be accepted or declined by the author of the recruitment post. The Community screens are available to members only and are not exposed to search engines. However, the title, cover photo and author nickname of some posts may be visible even to non-members in the "Popular travel stories" and "Find travel companions" previews on the home screen. The author of a post can edit its title, body and photos after posting (the destination, category and attached itinerary cannot be changed after posting) and can delete it.

The Service applies a zero-tolerance policy toward objectionable content and abuse of users. Since October 5, 2026, the Service does not automatically screen (for example with AI) posts, comments or companion posts before they are published; the operator makes decisions based on user reports. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours, and when the number of reports reaches a certain level, the content may be hidden before review. Companion group chat messages are handled in the same way: action is taken when reports accumulate or the operator confirms a violation. If review confirms a violation of these Terms, measures such as deleting the content or restricting the account may be taken. When you block another user, your posts and comments immediately become invisible to each other.
