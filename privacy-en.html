This policy explains openly how the Service processes personal information: the items processed, the purposes, the retention periods and users' rights.

2. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • Identifying members, keeping them signed in, and automatically syncing itineraries across devices
  • Providing storage, viewing and editing of trip itineraries
  • Co-editing the same itinerary with companions invited through a share link, and reflecting changes in real time
  • Automatically recognizing booking documents (flight and accommodation confirmations) and reflecting them in the itinerary
  • Providing additional features such as map and place search, AI nearby recommendations and city introductions, and weather
  • Showing first-time visitors a display language suited to their country of access
  • Operating the Community boards (posting and editing posts, comments, replies and images; follows; likes and bookmarks; handling reports and blocks) and providing notifications about reactions to my posts and comments
  • Handling companion recruitment, applications and matching; providing chat for matched groups; mutual confirmation of group members through QR codes when meeting offline; and displaying the author's and applicant's age range and gender on companion recruitment posts and application screens (only where they chose to enter them)
  • Sending notice emails needed to use the Service, such as sign-up and account deletion confirmations (not for promotion), and providing the app's push notifications and notification list (upcoming departures and travel advisories for my trips, comments and replies, companion and co-editing news, moderation results)
  • Receiving and answering inquiries, and improving the Service
  • Connecting to partner services (flights, hotels, tours, etc.)
  • Analyzing usage statistics and checking errors — understanding where use gets blocked and where errors occur in order to improve the Service
  • Preventing misuse and harmful content (receiving user reports and operator review, and automatic hiding when reports accumulate — since October 5, 2026, posts are not automatically screened before publication by AI or similar), and ensuring stability of the Service (request rate limiting, etc.)
  • Member management and responding to inquiries — in the member management screen, the operator can see a member's email, sign-up date, age range and gender, the title, city and period of trips they created, and the time and country of their latest access (used only for handling inquiries, preventing misuse and operating the Service, and accessible only to operator accounts)

3. Retention and Use Period

Users' personal information is destroyed without delay when they delete their account or when the purpose of retention is achieved. However, unless the user deletes it themself, trip itinerary data linked to a signed-in account is kept for as long as the Service is used.

  • Account information (email, name, profile photo), age range and gender, and recent activity records: until account deletion (age range and gender can be removed at any time in Settings, and are deleted immediately when removed)
  • Trip itinerary data: until the trip owner deletes it or the owner deletes their account (content entered by co-editing members is part of the trip and is kept for the same period)
  • Booking document files and recognition results: until the user deletes them, the trip is deleted, or the account is deleted
  • Co-editing participation records: until the member leaves, the owner removes the member, or the trip is deleted
  • Push notification address (token): this device's token is deleted when you turn notifications off or sign out, deleted immediately when Apple reports it can no longer be used, and all tokens are deleted when you delete your account. Notification records are kept until the user deletes them or the account is deleted.
  • Community posts, comments and images: until the user deletes them or the account is deleted (excluding content removed for violating operating policies). Notification records are kept until the user deletes them, the related post or comment is deleted, or the account is deleted. Photos removed while editing a post may remain in storage for a while and are cleaned up regularly.
  • Companion recruitment posts, application messages and chat messages: until the recruitment post is deleted or the account is deleted. When you leave a group, your access to that group's chat (including past messages) disappears immediately.
  • Inquiries and screenshots: until account deletion
  • Server access logs (IP): kept for up to 3 months under the Protection of Communications Secrets Act and then destroyed
  • Deleted posts and cancelled companion recruitments: they disappear from the screen immediately and are kept in a separate archive for operational needs such as handling reports and resolving disputes (not shown to users and visible only to the operator). The retention period is 2 months from the date of deletion or cancellation; attached photo files are deleted first after 1 month, and after 2 months all post and comment records are deleted. They are deleted together when the author deletes their account, and comments made by other members are also deleted from the archive when that member deletes their account.
  • Account deletion: when you delete your account in Settings, the account, trip itineraries, booking document files and recognition results, posts, comments, companion finder records, inquiries and the other items above are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered (trips being co-edited are deleted together when the owner deletes their account).
  • Members whom the operator forcibly removed for violating the Terms: to prevent signing up or signing in again with the same email, the email address, name, reason for suspension and date and time of the measure are kept until the suspension is lifted. All other data is deleted immediately as described above.
  • Usage analytics and error information: kept until the purpose of improving the Service is achieved, and deleted on the user's request (see Section 10 below)

5. Processing of Location Information

The Service may use the browser's location API (iOS location services in the app, only while you use the app) through the "current location" button on the map screen. This location information is used only inside the browser to move the map to the user's current location, and is not sent to a server or stored. Providing location is entirely the user's choice, and you can revoke the location permission at any time in the browser (in iOS Settings for the app).

Separately, when a signed-in member opens the app, the country of access (country level) that can be known from the access request is stored together with the time of the latest access. The exact location (latitude and longitude) and the IP address are not stored, and this is visible only to the operator for member management and misuse prevention.

6. Processing of Companion Matching Information

Because the companion finder is a feature premised on matching with other users, we explain it separately as follows.

  • When a match is confirmed, only members of the same group can see each other's community profile (nickname and profile photo).
  • The QR code used for offline meetings contains no personal information such as real name or contact details, only a randomly generated confirmation code. Scanning a QR code only confirms "whether the person is a member of the same matched group"; if someone who is not in the same group scans it, no information is revealed.
  • Group chat messages can be read only by members of that group, and when you leave the group, your access to the chat and your QR code expire immediately.
  • The age range and gender that a member chooses to enter are displayed next to the nickname of the author or applicant on companion recruitment posts and application screens. Only those of members who wrote a post or applied in the companion finder are visible to other signed-in members, and they are not disclosed together with other information such as email. If you do not enter them, they are not displayed, and you can change or delete them at any time in Settings.
  • Companion recruitment posts, application messages and group chat messages are not automatically screened before publication, and are managed through the report and block features and operator review.

7. Trip Co-editing (Share Links)

  • When a trip owner sends a share link, the recipient can join that trip as an editing member only after signing in. People who are not signed in cannot view the itinerary through a share link.
  • Members of the same trip can see each other's nicknames, profile photos and roles, and can view and edit the itinerary, accommodation, flights and expenses together. When someone edits, it is reflected immediately on the other members' screens.
  • The original booking document files can be seen only by the person who uploaded them. However, booking information recognized and reflected in the itinerary (flight or accommodation names, dates and times, booking numbers, etc.) can be seen by members of the same trip.
  • Members can leave a trip at any time, and the owner can remove members. A member who leaves or is removed immediately loses access to the trip. If the owner cuts the link, only new joining is blocked; members who have already joined remain.

8. Community Visibility and Search Exposure

Since October 5, 2026, the Community (destination boards, the free board, posts, comments and the companion finder) screens are available only to signed-in members and are not exposed to search engines (Naver, Google, etc.). Comments are also blocked on the server so that only signed-in members can read them. Posts that search engines collected earlier may take some time to disappear from search results. However, in order to show the "Popular travel stories" and "Find travel companions" previews on the home screen, the title, cover photo and author nickname of some posts and recruitment posts may be visible even to people who are not signed in, and for this purpose post information can be read from the server without signing in. Announcements, update history and the user guide (including the sample PDF) can be seen by anyone and contain no personal information. Personal trip itineraries, companion chats and inquiries are not made public.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

The Service uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) for smooth use.

  • An authentication token to keep your sign-in session
  • Cache data so that recently viewed itineraries can be seen even offline
  • User settings such as display language and screen theme (dark mode)
  • The share link code you were trying to join while signing in (to continue joining after sign-in; it becomes invalid after 30 minutes)
  • An anonymous identifier for the usage analytics tool (stored only in this storage, without using cookies)
  • Screen settings such as notification read/deleted records, the time when the latest-activity record was last sent, and whether the map card is collapsed
  • Temporary trips created before signing in (stored only on this device, and moved to your account and removed from this device when you sign in)

The Service does not display advertising. The app (iOS) also does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track your activity in other companies' apps or websites. Partner sites that you reach by tapping a partner link may use their own cookies to measure performance. You can refuse cookie storage in your browser settings, but some features may then be limited.

10. Users' Rights and How to Exercise Them

Users can exercise the following rights at any time.

  • Viewing, editing and deleting the trip itineraries, booking documents and posts they registered
  • Leaving a trip that is being co-edited
  • Disconnecting the sign-in (account) link and requesting account deletion
  • Requesting access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of the processing of personal information

You can delete your account yourself in Settings. For other privacy-related requests, please contact customer support (contact form) in the Service or the contact below, and we will act without delay, except for periods set by law.
