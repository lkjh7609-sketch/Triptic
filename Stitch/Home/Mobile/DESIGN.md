## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted specifically for mobile-first Korean and global travel experiences, where clarity, tranquility, and friction-free momentum convert complex itineraries into effortless journeys. Travel planning often triggers cognitive overload with dense flight bookings, ambiguous hotel check-ins, and multi-user coordinations. This design system strips away frantic visual noise, delivering serene confidence through organic coastal hues, tactile card layering, and liquid glass interactions.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Oceanic Poise:** Dominated by deep oceanic teals (`#005C55` / `#0F766E`) reminiscent of tranquil coastal waters, balanced against warm mineral sands (`#F9FAF8`) and crisp white sheets.
- **Boutique Editorial Polish:** Rich photography pairs seamlessly with balanced type hierarchies and pill-shaped navigation, conveying the curated feel of a bespoke travel magazine.
- **Tactile Certainty:** Mobile portrait viewports (optimized for 390px baseline widths) utilize thumb-friendly glass capsule switchers, snappy interactive micro-presses, and card elevation tiers that invite exploration without friction.

### Design Movement: Tactile Card Minimalism & Liquid Glass
The design marries modern Scandinavian-Japanese card minimalism with contemporary iOS liquid glass. Surfaces feature clean white containers subtly elevated against warm, calm foundations. Glassmorphic capsule tabs provide floating tactile controls directly within the user's primary thumb zones.

## Colors

The palette establishes an oceanic visual grounding: deep, steady teals anchor primary commitments, crisp whites isolate tactical itineraries, and warm gold accents denote verified milestones and travel badges.

### Palette Implementation Rules
- **Primary (`#005C55` / `#0F766E`):** Primary action buttons, floating CTA anchors, active navigation indicators, and key operational nodes.
- **Teal Tints (`#F0FDFA` / `#CCFBF1`):** Soft contextual backgrounds for active filters, passenger pills, timeline step indicators, and progress meters.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#CEA65C`):** Editorial highlights, rating stars, featured destination tags, and bookmark markers.
- **Urgent Accent (`#EA580C`):** High-discount badges ("34% 특가"), countdown deadlines, and emergency flight status warnings.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F9FAF8`):** Organic mineral off-white canvas that ensures white card layers visually pop with organic warmth.
- **Text Layers:** `#0F172A` delivers high contrast against both white cards and sand foundations for Hangul character rendering; `#475569` supports metadata, dates, and auxiliary specs; `#94A3B8` provides silent structure for placeholder fields and dividers.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes bilingual harmony between Latin numerals/cities and Korean Hangul. In codebases, `Plus Jakarta Sans` is matched directly with `Pretendard` or system Korean typography (`Apple SD Gothic Neo` / `Malgun Gothic`), ensuring shared x-height, baseline alignment, and optical stroke thickness.

### Hierarchical Directives
- **Hero & Section Anchors:** Headlines across mobile screens maintain negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with bold 700 weights to prevent tall Korean syllables from looking loose.
- **Comfortable Reading Densities:** Body copy uses a line-height multiplier of `1.5` to `1.6`, allowing dense destination reviews and itinerary transit points to remain legible under rapid thumb scrolling.
- **Metadata & Indicators:** Flight numbers, pricing indicators (`189,400원~`), and timestamp badges feature crisp numeric tabular alignment with `label-md` or `label-sm`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry is tuned directly for mobile portrait viewports (390px base width, scaling seamlessly to 428px on larger smartphones and folding displays) while maintaining structured desktop elegance when expanded.

### Mobile Grid & Spatial Architecture
- **Mobile Screen Width:** Strict `16px` (`margin-mobile: 1rem`) screen margins ensure maximum edge-to-edge content usage without edge clipping.
- **Horizontal Carousels & Snap Rails:** Multi-card discovery sections (e.g. Lowest Price Flights, Popular Travelogues) utilize bleeding horizontal scroll lists with `12px` inter-card gaps, snapping smoothly along the card edge.
- **Thumb-Zone Safety:** Primary action modules (e.g., "일정 편집하기", "대화하기", "무료로 시작하기") reside within the lower 40% thumb zone, either docked with `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` or integrated into floating frosted capsules.
- **Internal Card Rhythm:** Travel cards maintain internal padding of `14px` to `16px` (`space-lg`), with `8px` between metadata lines and `12px` separating imagery from card content blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) backed by oceanic and slate ambient shadows, combined with liquid glass backdrops on floating controls.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background `#F9FAF8`. Completely flat, non-reflective mineral substrate.
- **Level 1 (Standard Card Container):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Thumb Rails):** Lifted interactive cards and elevated bottom sheets. Shadow: `0 8px 18px -4px rgba(0, 92, 85, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Liquid Glass Capsules & Overlays):** Floating category selector capsule and modal controls. Backdrop blur `16px` with semi-translucent container (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` or dark hero pill `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`), bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` and a diffused drop shadow `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system blends rounded card structures with pill-shaped action touchpoints, creating an organic, human-friendly travel companion aesthetic.

### Shape Rationale
- **Primary Shells & Modals (`16px` / `rounded-lg`):** Standard travel booking cards, community matching cards, and workflow step cards. Top image masks follow the exact parent radius (`16px 16px 0 0`).
- **Input Fields & Secondary Actions (`10px` to `12px`):** Inline forms, search filters, and verification badges.
- **Liquid Glass Capsules & Interactive Pills (`9999px` / `rounded-full`):** Category switchers ("항공", "호텔", "투어"), tag filters, profile badges, and bottom floating action pills.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Tactile Action Triggers
- **Primary Floating Action Button:** Solid `#0F766E` background, `#FFFFFF` bold typography, `10px` or `rounded-full` radius. Minimum touch target height: `44px` (ideal `48px` on mobile). Active touch state scales down (`scale(0.97)` with `120ms ease-out`).
- **Ghost & Secondary Outlined Buttons:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, text `#0F766E`. Active press state shifts surface to `#F0FDFA`.
- **Icon Utility Navigators:** Circular `36px x 36px` or `40px x 40px` tap targets with hairline borders, housing slate `#475569` chevrons and bookmark icons.

### 2. Liquid Glass Capsule Switcher
- Floating navigation control positioned on hero backdrops or fixed viewport tops.
- Container: `rounded-full`, backdrop blur `12px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` on dark imagery or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` on light content.
- Selected Pill: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with deep slate `#0F172A` text, elevated with `0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`.
- Unselected Pills: Translucent background, soft white or slate text (`#475569`).

### 3. Destination & Deal Cards
- **Structure:** Edge-to-edge vertical stacked layout inside a `16px` rounded white container.
- **Visual Header:** `16:10` aspect ratio photo with top `16px` rounded corners. Top left overlay hosts deal pill badges (`#EA580C` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-sm`, font-size `11px`).
- **Body Section:** Padded with `14px`. Contains destination headline (`headline-sm`), carrier routing specs (`body-sm`, `#475569`), date span, and right-aligned emphasized price highlight (`#0F766E`, bold `16px`).

### 4. Community Matching Cards ("같이 갈 사람 찾기")
- **Container:** `#FFFFFF` card with hairline border, padded with `14px 16px`.
- **Match Status Header:** Displays recruiting ratio (`모집중 1/2명`), age/gender preferences, and match accuracy badge (`98% 일치` in `#0F766E` with green pulse dot).
- **Body & Tags:** Itinerary preview paragraph followed by horizontal tag chips (`#미식탐방`, `#사진촬영`).
- **Action Footer:** Profile thumbnail alongside level verification (`소연 (Lv.4)`), paired with a compact primary conversation button ("대화하기").

### 5. Onboarding & Feature Steps ("트립틱 이렇게 써요")
- Segmented container with soft sand background (`#F1F5F2`), `16px` radius, and `16px` inner padding.
- Number indicator: Circular `26px x 26px` pill with `#CCFBF1` surface and `#005C55` bold index number.
- Timeline flow displays step milestones linked by dotted vertical hairline connectors (`#CBD5E1`).

### 6. Filter Chips & Pill Tags
- **Default State:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#475569`, padding `6px 14px`, `rounded-full`.
- **Active State:** Deep teal tint `#F0FDFA`, border `1.5px solid #0F766E`, text `#0F766E`, font weight 600.
